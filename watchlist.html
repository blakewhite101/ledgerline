Alerts are checked whenever you open Ledgerline, against prices that refresh every 30 minutes. With notifications on, your browser also shows a pop-up.

Prices come from public market feeds and refresh about every 30 minutes during market hours. Ledgerline is for general information only and is not investment advice.
