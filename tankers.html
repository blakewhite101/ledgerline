Chokepoints and busy waters

A lot of stopped tankers in one area can mean congestion, a backlog at port, or ships waiting out a disruption. Click an area to zoom the map to it.

Ship positions come from the free AISStream feed of coastal receivers and refresh every two hours, so ships far out at sea can show their last coastal position or be missing. Ships seen in the last 24 hours are shown. Map data from Natural Earth. Ledgerline is for general information only and is not investment advice.
